📊 Credit Risk Analysis Dashboard

The Credit Risk Analysis Dashboard is a data-driven project built using Power BI to analyze and evaluate borrower risk in lending systems. This dashboard helps in understanding customer behavior, loan distribution, and key financial indicators that influence loan approval decisions.

The primary aim of this project is to identify high-risk and low-risk borrowers by analyzing multiple factors such as income, credit history, loan intent, interest rates, and loan grades. It provides a clear and interactive way to explore data, making it easier for financial institutions to take informed decisions.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze patterns in loan approval and rejection
Identify risk factors affecting borrowers
Understand loan distribution across categories
Evaluate impact of credit history and income
Support data-driven decision-making in lending

📊 Insights & Analysis
🔹 Loan Intent Analysis

The dashboard shows how loans are distributed across categories such as education, medical, personal, venture, debt consolidation, and home improvement. Education and venture loans contribute a significant portion of total loans.

🔹 Interest Rate vs Loan Status

Higher interest rates are mostly associated with loans that are not approved or are riskier. Approved loans tend to have relatively lower interest rates, indicating better borrower profiles.

🔹 Credit History Impact

Borrowers with a longer credit history have a higher probability of loan approval. This highlights the importance of maintaining a good credit record.

🔹 Home Ownership Analysis

Most loans are taken by individuals living in rented or mortgaged properties. Borrowers who own homes represent a smaller share, suggesting different financial behaviors.

🔹 Loan Grade Distribution

Loan grades A and B dominate the dataset, indicating that a large portion of loans are given to low-risk borrowers. Lower grades (E, F, G) contribute less, reflecting cautious lending.
